# 🌌 MekPack: Modpack Design Philosophy & Vision

## 1. Executive Summary

Most modern tech modpacks suffer from a critical flaw: **disparate mods undermining each other's progression**. 
When Applied Energistics 2, Pipez, ComputerCraft, and wireless dimensional storage mods use trivial vanilla ingredients (iron, glass, redstone), the player can rush end-game digital automation, instant item transfer, and interdimensional logistics before ever laying down an industrial smelter or building an energy grid.

**MekPack: Future Frontiers** takes a radically different design approach:
**Mekanism is the Sovereign Spine of the entire modpack.**

Every single companion mod in MekPack exists to **amplify, extend, and interface with Mekanism**, rather than compete with or bypass it.

---

## 2. Core Tenets

### Tenet I: Unified Industrial Spine
Mekanism's chemical and metallurgical processing chain is one of the most intricately balanced progression ladders in Minecraft modding:
- Metallurgic Infusion (`Enriched Iron` -> `Steel` -> `Infused Alloy` -> `Reinforced Alloy` -> `Atomic Alloy`)
- Circuit Fabrication (`Basic` -> `Advanced` -> `Elite` -> `Ultimate`)
- Chemical Ore Multiplication (2x -> 3x -> 4x -> 5x)
- Nuclear Engineering (Fission -> Turbine -> Fusion -> SPS Antimatter)

In MekPack, every high-tech machine, pipe upgrade, wireless transmitter, and supercomputer terminal requires corresponding tier materials from this industrial ladder.

### Tenet II: Earned Convenience
Convenience mods are essential for large-scale automation, but unearned convenience drains the joy of progression.
- **Pipez**: Provides hyper-fast routing, but its high-throughput upgrades require Mekanism Control Circuits and Alloys.
- **DimStorage & Entangled**: Instant wireless cross-dimensional transfer is an extraordinary power that requires Mekanism Teleportation Cores, Refined Obsidian, and Atomic Alloys.
- **Shrink**: Quantum player miniaturization is an endgame research wonder requiring antimatter or teleportation technology.
- **Chunk Loaders**: Continuous factory loading scales with Mekanism energy and tier circuits.

### Tenet III: Symbiotic Inter-Mod Synergy
Companion mods are treated as specialized subsystems of the Mekanism megastructure:
- **Applied Energistics 2 (AE2)**: Acts as the high-level digital brain and autocrafting conductor for Mekanism's chemical and processing factories.
- **CC: Tweaked**: Serves as the industrial control room, running Lua telemetry dashboards and safety monitors for Mekanism Fission and Fusion reactors.
- **SecurityCraft**: Protects high-tech industrial compounds and radioactive containment vaults with reinforced steel doors, laser grids, and retinal scanners.
- **Moving Elevators**: Provides realistic multi-floor transportation inside sprawling skyscraper factories and Lost Cities urban reclamation facilities.

### Tenet IV: The 0 to Endgame Journey
A player's journey through MekPack is divided into 6 distinct, rewarding technological eras:
1. **Tier 0: Ground Zero (Osmium Prospecting & The Primitive Spark)**
2. **Tier 1: The Age of Steel & Infusion (Metallurgic Infuser & Early Logistics)**
3. **Tier 2: Kinetic Automation & Digital Inception (Enrichment, AE2 Starters, Tier Upgrades)**
4. **Tier 3: Chemical Mastery & Quantum Networking (Purification, AE2 Autocrafting, DimStorage)**
5. **Tier 4: Nuclear Splendor & Spatial Entanglement (Fission, Industrial Turbine, Entangled, Wormholes)**
6. **Tier 5: The Supercritical Frontier (Fusion, SPS Antimatter, MekaSuit, Post-Scarcity QIO)**

By enforcing this cohesive architecture, completing each milestone feels earned, cinematic, and profoundly satisfying.
