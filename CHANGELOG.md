# Changelog

## 0.0.3 - 2026-11-27

### What's New
- Players now automatically receive the Theseus Quest Book in their inventory when joining a world.

### What's Changed
- Removed the old MekPack advancement tree and the advancements shortcut button from the player inventory screen so progression stays centered on the Theseus Quest Book.

### What's Fixed
- Fixed custom MekPack recipes for Applied Energistics 2 and other companion mods being overridden by default mod recipes.

## 0.0.2 - 2026-11-27

### What's New
- Added Theseus Quests with a 6-chapter Sovereign Spine quest book covering 33 progression milestones from Osmium mining to Antimatter synthesis.
- Added item and experience rewards across all six chapters, including branching quest wings for Applied Energistics 2, Pipez, CC: Tweaked, SecurityCraft, DimStorage, Entangled, Wormhole, Moving Elevators, and Shrink.

### What's Changed
- Updated Mekanism and its Generators, Tools, and Additions modules to the latest Minecraft 26.3 development build, including fixes for the Digital Miner, Induction Matrix power export, and Industrial Turbine output scaling.
- Updated NeoForge to 26.3.0.110.
- Updated companion mods including Applied Energistics 2, CC: Tweaked, JEI, DimStorage, Entangled, Trash Cans, Curios, Sodium, Lithium, Entity Culling, More Culling, and The Lost Cities.

## 0.0.1 - 2026-10-26

### What's New
- Initial Minecraft 26.3 NeoForge release centered around Mekanism 10.8 with six progression tiers.
- Integrated companion tech, logistics, security, and world generation mods with custom recipes tied to Mekanism progression.
- Added a 33-milestone progression tree with Better Advancements, Advancement Plaques, and custom reward loot tables.
